🚴‍♂️ AdventureWorks Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive, multi-page Microsoft Power BI solution built to analyze global sales, regional distribution, product trends, and customer demographics for a cycling equipment manufacturer. Developed as part of the hands-on **Microsoft Power BI Desktop Course** by Honhaar Jawan.

📌 Project Overview:
The dashboard provides executive leadership with actionable insights into overall business performance, geographic sales density, subcategory trends, and customer purchasing patterns.

* Executive Analytics: Tracks core KPIs including sales, revenue, profit, and product return rates.
* Geographical Mapping:Visualizes order distribution across international markets.
* Product & Customer Profiling: Identifies top-selling product categories, target vs. actual goals, and high-value customers.

🛠️ Data Architecture & Tech Stack:

* Business Intelligence: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* ETL & Data Transformation: Power Query
* Calculations & Logic: Dynamic DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for metrics, KPI targets, and return rates
* Data Modeling: Star Schema Relational Data Model

📊 Dashboard Pages & Insights

1. Executive Page

* Core KPIs: $24.9M Total Revenue, 25.2K Total Orders, $312.1K Profit, and 0.5% Return Rate.
* Category Volume: Accessories leads order volume (17.0K), followed by Bikes (13.9K) and Clothing (7.0K).
* Key Highlights: Tires and Tubes is the top-selling subcategory; Bib-Shorts records the highest return rate.

2. Maps Page

* Global Footprint: Interactive dark-mode bubble map tracking orders across North America (US, Canada), Europe (UK, Germany, France), and Pacific (Australia).
* Interactivity: One-click region slicers for continent-level filtering.

3. Product and Customer Page

* Customer Baseline: Analyzes 17.4K unique customers averaging $1.4K revenue per customer.
* Top Customer: Mr. Maurice Shan leads with $12.4K in total revenue across 6 orders.
* Demographics: Income-level breakdown highlighting customer segments (*Average: 12K, Low: 10K, High: 3K).

💡 Key Takeaways

* Revenue Drivers: Low-cost accessories generate the highest overall transaction volume.
* Quality Assurance: High return rates in specific soft goods (Bib-Shorts) highlight potential sizing or vendor quality optimization areas.
* Customer Loyalty: Repeat high-value customers contribute disproportionately to total revenue, emphasizing the value of retention programs.
